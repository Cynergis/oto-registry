# Capturing the core of a product

Ask these in order. Each answer becomes facts in the vocabulary named after it.

## Why does it exist? What is wrong today, and where is it going?
A `Product` with its problem, background, vision, domain, value stream and stage, `owned_by` a `Role`.

## Who owns it, who decides, who must be consulted?
`Role` nodes with kind owner, decider, stakeholder or contributor, a mandate, and what each is `interested_in`.

## Who is it for, and who is it explicitly not for?
`Persona` nodes, target or excluded, each with the need it is trying to meet.

## What is the promise, in one sentence, and how will we know it was kept?
A `ValueProposition` the product `offers`, `intended_for` a persona; a `SuccessCriterion` with a metric, a target and a date that `measures` it.

## What must it do, and which promise does each need serve?
`Requirement` nodes with kind and priority, each `serves` a value proposition.

## How is each requirement accepted?
A `Scenario` with given, when and then that `exercises` it.

## What ships first, and what is explicitly out?
A `Release` with its target date, state and horizon; requirements `in_scope_of` or `excluded_from` it.

## What was decided, by whom, and why? What is still open?
`Decision` nodes `decided_by` a role, `about` their subject, each `documented_in` a `Document`; `OpenQuestion` nodes a decision later `resolves`.
