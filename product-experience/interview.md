# Capturing the experience of a product

Ask these after the product-core interview.

## What are we trying to achieve this period, and what higher goal does it support?
`Objective` nodes with a period and a state; one `supports` another; a value proposition `serves` one.

## What is the product made of?
`Feature` nodes `part_of` the product or a parent feature; each `realises` requirements.

## How does a persona get the value, step by step?
A `UserJourney` with its trigger and outcome, `intended_for` a persona; `JourneyStep` nodes `part_of` it, in order.

## What does this word mean here?
`Term` nodes with a definition that `defines` the entity they name.
